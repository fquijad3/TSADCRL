# Time-Series-Anomaly-Detection-Models-with-Contrastive-Representation-Learning
"Time Series Anomaly Detection Models with Contrastive Representation Learning" which presents a time series contrastive representation learning method for anomaly detection. Important model differences are compared for time series to evaluate the impact on anomaly detection, namely, the form of the encoder and whether supervised fine-tuning is applied. Different representations are obtained, frozen ones from unsupervised training and liquid representations produced with self-supervised learning. Also, an empirical procedure is implemented to detect anomalies. The method is applied over sensor-based data from human activities recognition, ECG readings and industrial processes.
